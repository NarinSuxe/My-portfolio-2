01. ABOUT ME

Turning ideas into playable experiences.

Hi! I'm Narin, a UX Engineer student from Sweden who loves gaming, creativity, and technology. I enjoy creating digital experiences that feel both useful and fun, and I'd love to bring UX and gaming together in the future.

I work with Figma, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I'm always looking for new ways to improve my design and coding skills. What I enjoy most about UX is solving problems, exploring ideas, and finding ways to make things easier and more enjoyable to use.

Accessibility is also an important part of how I approach design. My own experience with visual accessibility gives me a different perspective on how people interact with digital products, and I want to use that perspective to create experiences that work for everyone.

I'm curious, creative, and always ready to experiment with new ideas. My goal is to create digital experiences that are useful, engaging, and enjoyable to use.
